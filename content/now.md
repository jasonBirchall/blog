than a feed of everything I've ever done. Derek Sivers
[started the idea](https://nownownow.com/about), and I liked it enough to steal it.

while working out how browser extensions actually ship. I wrote up that whole
saga in [[publishing-a-browser-extension-in-2026]] if you want the long version.

The numbers below update themselves once a day, straight from my editor. The
words up here I write by hand, and I change them when the shape of the work
changes -- which is to say, not often. If the stats look stale, that probably
just means I've been reading more than typing.
